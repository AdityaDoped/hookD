Privacy

The short version: your songs never leave your device. Hookd has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics.

  • Your songs and mashups are opened, mixed and saved entirely inside your browser. They are never uploaded to Hookd or anyone else.
  • What's saved on your device: so a refresh doesn't lose your work, Hookd keeps your songs and settings in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). Only this browser on this device can read them. Tap Start over or clear this site's data in your browser settings to delete them.
  • What other services see: to load the page, your browser connects to the host (Vercel), to Google Fonts for the typefaces, and to jsDelivr and cdnjs (Cloudflare) for open-source code libraries. Like any website request, these services receive your IP address and basic browser details, and they may keep short-term logs under their own privacy policies. None of them receive your songs.
  • Automatic captions: only if you use them, your browser downloads a speech-recognition model (Whisper) from Hugging Face once and keeps it. The listening happens on your device; your video's sound is never sent to Hugging Face or anyone else.
  • Vocals only / Beat only: only if you use them, your browser downloads a vocal-separation model (Demucs, about 170 MB) from Hugging Face once and keeps it. The separating happens on your device; your songs are never sent anywhere.
  • Match keys: only if you turn it on, your browser downloads a small pitch-shifting library from jsDelivr. Your songs stay on your device.
  • What we collect: nothing. Hookd doesn't run a server or a database, so there's nothing about you for us to store, sell or share.

Copyright & your music

  • Hookd doesn't host, provide, sell or distribute any music. It's a tool, like an audio editor. Every song comes from the person using it, stays on their device, and is never sent to us.
  • A mashup is still built from other people's work. Songs are usually protected by copyright, so only use music you own or have permission to use. Making a mashup for yourself and posting one publicly are different things. Publishing may need permission from the rights holders.
  • Posting on social media: platforms such as Instagram and YouTube have their own music licensing and copyright systems. They may mute, block or label content that uses copyrighted music. That's between you and the platform.
  • Copyright complaints: because no music is stored or shared through Hookd, there's no uploaded content for us to remove. If you believe something that is part of this website (its code, text or images) infringes your rights, please get in touch using the contact details below.

Open source

Hookd's code is open source under the MIT License. It uses these libraries and fonts, each under its own license:

  • lamejs for MP3 encoding (LGPL, loaded unmodified)
  • mp4-muxer and webm-muxer for video files (MIT)
  • Transformers.js and ONNX Runtime for automatic captions (Apache 2.0 and MIT), running OpenAI's Whisper speech model (MIT)
  • Meta's Demucs (HTDemucs v4) for Vocals only and Beat only (MIT), in the ONNX export by the WebNN team (MIT), running on ONNX Runtime (MIT)
  • Signalsmith Stretch for key matching (MIT)
  • Yatra One, Bricolage Grotesque, Hind, Figtree, JetBrains Mono, Lato, Poppins and Playfair Display from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License)
